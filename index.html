Frequently asked questions

Common questions about OluwaConvert.

What is the minimum conversion amount?

The minimum conversion amount is ₦100.

Which networks are supported?

The current demo supports MTN, Airtel, Glo and 9mobile.

Which banks are supported?

The converter includes a broad list of Nigerian banks and financial institutions. Actual payout availability will depend on the payment provider connected to the production system.

Are the displayed rates guaranteed?

No. The displayed rates are demo rates for the current version of the website. Production rates may change.

Is the payout system currently live?

No. The current public version is a demo. Automated airtime/data verification and bank payouts require the necessary provider and payment integrations.

What happens after I submit a request?

The request is recorded with a transaction reference and can be reviewed through the administration system. Production verification and payout require the appropriate external integrations.

Terms & Conditions

OluwaConvert is currently provided as a demonstration platform. Conversion estimates, displayed rates, transaction records and workflow examples are for demonstration purposes unless and until the required production infrastructure is connected.

Users should not transfer airtime, data or funds to OluwaConvert solely on the basis of this demonstration website. A live service will provide specific instructions, applicable fees, processing conditions, eligibility requirements and payout terms before customers use the production service.

The minimum conversion amount shown on this website is ₦100. Displayed rates are examples and may change in a production environment.

Privacy Policy

The demonstration converter collects information entered into the form, such as network, transaction amount, phone number and bank account details, for the purpose of demonstrating the transaction workflow.

Only the information necessary for the demonstrated transaction workflow should be submitted. The production service will provide a complete privacy policy describing data collection, storage, security, retention, sharing and user rights before live customer operations begin.

Do not submit sensitive information that is not required by the converter.
